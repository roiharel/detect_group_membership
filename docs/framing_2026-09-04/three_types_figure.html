Three ways a group boundary opens, each with one continuous depth

Figure 1. The three social event types, defined by who crosses. Left of each panel: a worked example from the tracking record — rows are collared animals, columns hours (nights in c), cell colour is the animal's social context, and unobserved animal-intervals are left blank so observation and behaviour stay separable. Right: the distribution of that type's depth measure over every event, all three on a common 0–1 scale, with the median and its bootstrap interval. The three depth distributions have three characteristically different shapes, and that is the point of the panel. (a) Between-group: two units share one cluster; depth is mutual participation. Median 1.00, IQR 0.75–1.00, n = 1,705 encounters — and 57% sit at exactly 1.0 against 17% below half. Between-group opening is largely all-or-nothing: when two units meet, most of both units are usually in it. (b) Within-group: one unit holds more than one cluster; depth is the share of the unit outside its own largest cluster. Median 0.12, IQR 0.08–0.27, n = 13,751 unit-hours — low and continuous, so a unit that divides usually detaches a small minority rather than halving. (c) Individual: one animal leaves its unit; depth is the share of away-nights spent with another unit. Median 0.55, n = 338 excursions, and strongly bimodal — 48% at 0 and 49% at 1. An animal is either alone for the whole trip or with another unit for the whole trip; passing through both is rare.

This replaces a six-type taxonomy that split merges into large, medium-partial and small-subset. Two reasons. The saved merge_scale label is assigned from the maximum participation a unit ever reaches rather than its typical state, so events labelled small-subset can have maximum fractions near 1.0. And the subdivision does not distinguish groups: between-group ICC 0.05–0.13 with permutation p = 0.09–0.26, and well-sampled groups traverse most of its range within their own record. It is a gradient measured on a faulty rule and reported as three categories, so it is better carried as the continuous depth in panel (a). Examples: Chartreuse - Purple, 2025-08-08; depth 0.69; Lapis, 2026-07-06; depth 0.44; 24AE29 of Lapis, 2025-08-09; depth 0.90. All panels and depth distributions come from the frozen 1,924,104-row export (2024-03-01 to 2026-07-22); within-group depth is computed over unit-hours with at least 8 observed animals.
