How GPS fixes become dynamic social units

Methods figure. The clustering chain and what each step keeps. (a) Positions are clustered within each hour by adaptive clustering over a 120–900 m edge range, giving one spatial cluster per hour (temp_group_id, median 12 animals). Consecutive hours of the same cluster stitch into an association event (association_event_id, 20,342 events), which is the Stage-1 encounter identity. Reading a cluster's composition against the animals' origin labels gives a social context; contexts resolve into an assigned unit recomputed every hour; and a sustained-association rule collapses those into the dynamic unit used for analysis. The assigned label carries 26 units + ISOLATED + 22 `mixed:` composites — a merge shows up as a mixed: composite rather than as one of the units. (b) Spell lengths, log axis, with 95% cluster-bootstrap bands resampling animals. An assigned-unit spell has a median of 7 h across 41,279 spells; a dynamic-unit spell 4,323 h across 379 — a 618-fold difference. 79.9% of animal-hours sit in an assigned spell shorter than a day, against 0.8% for dynamic. The rule is not a smoothing choice at the margin; it discards essentially all of the hourly structure. (c) The same resolution for one animal — 24AA01 of Bronze — over 14 days at hourly resolution. Cluster size moves between 1 and 50; social context takes 4 distinct values; the assigned label takes 6, spending 68 h in mixed: composites; and the dynamic unit takes 1 value for the whole window.

Panel (b) counts a spell as a run of constant label for one animal, so a spell ends when the label changes or the animal's record does; spells are right-censored at the ends of the record, which affects the dynamic curve far more than the assigned one and makes the 618-fold ratio a lower bound. Panel (c) uses hours whose state is fixed or carried overnight. Choosing between these identifiers is not cosmetic: temp_group_id cannot persist across hours, association_cluster_id is the join key for fine-scale 2-minute rows, and association_event_id is the only one that counts events — the full mapping is in phase0_cohort_ledger/event_id_crosswalk.csv. Frozen export, 1,924,104 animal-hours, 2024-03-01 to 2026-07-22. Generated by build_pipeline_figure.py; seed 20260904.
