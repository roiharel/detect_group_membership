# Rebuild on the 2026-09-05 canonical data

## What was rebuilt, what it changed, and one bug it exposed

Everything from the GPS file to Figures 1 and 2 was rebuilt by
`rebuild_all_20260905.py`, a fifteen-step driver that logs each step and stops on the
first failure. The frozen vintage is archived, not overwritten, at
`outputs/general_structure_2026_09_frozen20260722` and
`outputs/membership_export_narrow_frozen20260722`, so both remain reproducible.

Three things changed at once, deliberately, and the comparison below separates them.

| | frozen 2026-07-22 | rebuild 2026-09-05 |
|---|---|---|
| **data** | canonical share as of 2026-07-22 | canonical share as of **2026-09-05**: 30,143,804 fixes, 392 animals, through 2026-09-05 |
| **coverage** | `--min-fixes 3` | **`--min-fixes 1`** |
| **inference** | none | **interpolation of fix-free hours**, ceiling scaled to each collar's own fix rate |

Everything else is pinned to the frozen run — `local_2h` support, seven-day dynamic-unit
rule, sparse-origin guard at 3 animals, adaptive k=2 factor 1.65 clipped 120–900 m.

---

## 1. The coverage rule mattered far more than the interpolation

The question that started this was how to include the 22 collars that fix only every two
hours. The answer turned out not to be interpolation.

| support type | rows | share |
|---|---:|---:|
| observed_exact_hour | 1,547,866 | 58.1% |
| carried_night | 648,378 | 24.3% |
| local_2h_window | 459,656 | 17.2% |
| **interpolated** | **9,833** | **0.37%** |

`--min-fixes 1` alone recovered **409,196 observed animal-hours (+36%)** that a three-fix
median threshold had been discarding — 57,019 of them belonging to the sparse collars, and
**318,242 belonging to the dense ones**, whose partial hours were being thrown away for no
stated reason. The pre-existing `local_2h` support then covers the alternate hour a
two-hourly collar leaves empty, and its row count nearly tripled (179,736 → 459,656).

So the honest claim for the paper is **"single-fix hours are no longer discarded"**, not
"sparse collars were interpolated in". Only 0.37% of rows rest on an inferred position,
each flagged `position_support_type='interpolated'`, `is_observed=False`, and treated as a
passenger in the clustering: excluded from the kNN scale, unable to act as a
single-linkage bridge, attached to the cluster of its nearest observed neighbour.

### The interpolation ceiling is cadence-aware, and it has to be

A flat ceiling is wrong in both directions. A collar fixing every two hours leaves an
empty hour *by design*; a collar fixing every two minutes that goes quiet for six hours
has *failed*. The ceiling is therefore three times the animal's own natural spacing,
capped at 6 h — which resolves to **6 h for sparse collars and 3 h for dense ones**.

A first attempt at this silently did nothing: it measured each animal's spacing from the
supported hourly table, but `local_2h` has already made that table roughly hourly for
everybody, so every animal came out at 1 h and received an identical ceiling. Cadence has
to be read from **fixes per hour** — about 30 for a two-minute collar, 1 for a two-hourly
one — not from row spacing.

### What interpolation cannot fix, and should not be asked to

A census of every hole inside an animal's tracking span:

| ceiling | missing hours recovered | share of all missing |
|---|---:|---:|
| ≤2 h | 4,824 | 3.3% |
| ≤6 h | 9,144 | 6.3% |
| ≤12 h | 25,424 | 17.4% |
| ≤24 h | 38,193 | 26.1% |
| unlimited | 146,066 | 100% |

**146,066 animal-hours are missing inside tracking spans, and 45% of them sit in gaps
longer than a week.** Twenty-seven animals remain below 0.85 coverage of their own span,
nine below 0.50. Those are collar failures. Filling them would be fabrication, and they
are left empty.

## 2. A bug the improved coverage exposed

`build_event_examples_figure.py` failed with "no gapless window found". The cause was a
predicate used in five scripts and documented in the figure's own header:

```
known state = is_observed | is_carried_night          # "96.3% of animal-hours"
```

That predicate covers **82.6%** of the new table, and at **17:00 it covers almost
nothing**. The frozen build filled 17:00 with 79,195 `carried_night` rows; the new build
fills it with **106,147 `local_2h_window` rows carrying `is_observed=False`**. Since the
figure's window spans 17:00, no window could ever be gapless.

Accepting a position carried across the night while rejecting one borrowed from a real fix
sixty minutes away was indefensible — the borrowed position is the better evidence. The
predicate is now:

```
known state = is_observed | is_carried_night | is_local_2h_supported
```

in `analyze_individual_axis_identifiability.py`, `derive_excursion_destinations.py`,
`derive_level2_inputs.py`, `build_event_examples_figure.py` and
`build_pipeline_figure.py`. Interpolated rows are deliberately **not** counted as known:
their position is inferred, not observed. Three steps that had already run under the old
predicate were re-run.

**The general lesson:** a predicate that names two of three ways a row can carry a
position was silently correct only while the third was rare. Improving coverage changed
which mechanism supplies a given hour, and the predicate broke without any code changing.

## 3. What moved, measured rather than asserted

`compare_vintages.py` restricts to the overlap window and to animals present in both, so
that "more animals and more days" cannot explain everything.

| product | frozen | rebuild | ratio |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| membership rows | 1,924,104 | 2,665,733 | ×1.39 |
| animals | 350 | **392** | +42, **none lost** |
| origin groups / dynamic units | 26 / 26 | 27 / 27 | |
| opportunity dyad-days | 73,353 | 96,106 | ×1.31 |
| stage-1 events | 1,705 | 2,457 | ×1.44 |
| excursions | 338 | 425 | ×1.26 |
| weekly network metrics | 1,268 | 1,596 | ×1.26 |
| transfer edges | 18 | **29** | ×1.61 |

On the **1,922,637 animal-hours present in both vintages**:

* **`dynamic_social_unit` agreement 99.7%** — group membership, the load-bearing variable
  for most of the paper, is essentially unchanged.
* **`social_context` agreement 91.9%**, and the disagreements are systematic:

| reclassification | rows |
|---|---:|
| `with_origin` → `mixed_with_origin_present` | 78,400 |
| `other` → `with_origin` | 27,477 |
| `isolated` → `with_origin` | 12,364 |
| `isolated` → `other` | 8,880 |

**All of it points one way: more collars means more company detected.** An animal formerly
scored alone with its own group is now in a cluster that also holds another group's
animals; animals formerly scored away or isolated are now found with their own group,
because a groupmate that had no fix in that hour now has one. `carried_night` *fell*
(725,223 → 648,378) despite 39% more rows — real observations displaced assumptions.

### The consequence for the manuscript

**Between-group merge rates will rise and isolation rates will fall, and not because
behaviour changed.** Stage-1 events grew ×1.44, faster than the underlying rows at ×1.39;
transfer edges grew ×1.61. Every axis-A and axis-C rate quoted from the frozen vintage
must be restated against the new one, and **cross-vintage comparisons of those rates are
not valid**. This is the coverage confound the seasonality work already flagged, now with
a magnitude attached to it.

Also to check before it supports any claim: there are now **27 origin groups and 27
dynamic units** against 26/26. The extra unit's collar count should be confirmed — a unit
with one collar cannot carry a group-level statement.

## 4. Still open

1. **Cluster hourly partitions from 2-minute distances** rather than from hourly median
   positions. Already measured as better on all four criteria in both validation windows
   (silhouette 0.941→0.955, persistence 0.978→1.000, collar-count bias 0.54→0.45), and
   there is a median of 30 shared 2-minute bins per dyad-hour that the median currently
   discards. Deferred because it invalidates every `association_event_id`.
2. **A 2-minute duration axis** for panel 2d — see
   [TWO_MINUTE_DURATION_AXIS.md](TWO_MINUTE_DURATION_AXIS.md). Pilot written
   (`build_2min_pilot.py`), to be run on one month before anything larger.

Both invalidate the same cached identifiers, so they should be done as **one** further
rebuild rather than two.
