What modularity looks like

Figure 3a. Within-group modularity for one group, through time and as pictures. Left: Lilac week by week, across its 38 weeks at 12 or more collars (2025-07-28 to 2026-04-27); bar height is that week’s modularity, dots are weeks at zero, and the teal trace is the largest sub-community as a share of the group on the right-hand axis. 21 of 38 weeks are modular, in 5 shaded phases of two weeks or more, the longest running 7 consecutive weeks; up to 3 communities appear and the largest falls to 42%. The group does not drift apart and stay apart — it separates and re-fuses, on a scale of weeks. Right: the two marked weeks as association networks. At Q = 0.45 (2025-08-04) the group is two communities that barely associate; at Q = -0.00 (2026-03-30) it is one connected mass with no partition to find, on the same 12 collars. Both are the same unit five months apart, which is the case for reading modularity as a state rather than a trait. It is also not a typical unit: across the 7 units with at least 20 weeks at 12 or more collars, 5 reach modularity in some week and 2 never leave zero, but Lilac and Chartreuse alone hold 37 of the 42 modular weeks (88%). This range is what a unit can do, not what most units do.

Everything here is restricted to well-covered unit-weeks. Below about 12 collars a modularity estimator returns a single community regardless of the animals, so a low score from a sparse unit is a statement about the collars, not the baboons; the 2 silent units counted in the caption are all well-covered and their zeros are real. The two networks are matched on collar count, so the flat one cannot be dismissed as a thinner week. Both are the week’s association network: an edge is the share of hours two animals spent in the same association cluster, among hours both were present, drawn at 0.05 and above; communities are greedy modularity communities on that weighted graph, and positions come from a spring layout with a fixed seed and each community’s centre then placed on a fixed circle, so that both networks are drawn to one convention and distance on the page is indicative rather than metric. Weekly metrics come from the frozen 1,924,104-row export; networks are built by derive_level2_inputs.py. Generated by build_modularity_figure.py; seed 20260904.
