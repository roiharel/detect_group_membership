01  /  The two definitions

One asks whether a 5 m contact was recorded; the other asks whether two groups shared a cluster

The meeting model — response as fitted

0.21%

117 positive dyad-days in 56,128 candidates across 207 dyads. A candidate is any pair of groups each tracked somewhere that day, so the median “opportunity to meet” is two groups 10.7 km apart — the maximum is 37.9 km. A positive requires a recorded 5 m cross-group edge, and on positive days the groups are a median of 440 m apart.

The structural merge — inventory as built

3,217

merge events across 74 dyads. A merge exists when animals of two units belong to the same spatial cluster — adaptive clustering with a 120–900 m edge range, so the scale is hundreds of metres. There is no denominator at all, because it is an inventory rather than a model.

The two halves of the model are joined by a left merge and fillna(0), and that is where the artefact enters. Only 128 of the 56,128 candidate dyad-days appear in the fine-scale file at all; 117 of those are positive; the remaining 56,000 are zero-filled with eligible_2min_bins = 0, so there is no exposure behind the zero. The file itself is bigmerge_..._no_copper_lilac_2min_metric_rows.csv — the exclusion is in the filename, while the excluded dyad's days stay in the denominator as zeros.

Both measurements are legitimate. Using one as the response while meaning the other is what makes the ecological coefficients uninterpretable.

02  /  Phase 0 — cohort ledger

Twenty products, one frozen source, three corrected numbers

  • The legacy inventory source holds 1,703,133 rows and 347 animals. The 1,516,740 figure quoted from its metadata is rows_used after filtering to 2025 onward, not the source size.
  • The saved candidate table spans 207 dyads over 21 groups because it derives from the older proximity-status product, whose unit set includes SneakySilver but omits Gold, Green, Mint, Red and TrickyTeal. The frozen source has 26 units and 317 co-observed dyads.
  • The 2 m fine-scale product's file is named canonical_5m_shuffle_expectation_2min_rows.csv. Radius is set by the directory, not the filename — anything resolving it by name alone silently analyses the wrong radius.

The crosswalk records which identifier answers which question: temp_group_id for one hour and one cluster, association_cluster_id as the join key for fine-scale bins, association_event_id as Stage-1 encounter identity, and the legacy merge_episode_id and positive episode_id, which should now be used only to reproduce saved results.

04  /  Phase 2 — two-stage unit

Three durations, and a state that is not an encounter

1,705 Stage-1 events across 68 dyads, each carrying three durations that the saved pipeline reported as one figure: structural span (median 14 h), supported exposure (median 2.3 h), and active contact time (median 0.17 h).

Sixteen runs are sustained associations, in five dyads

Sixteen Stage-1 runs last at least 168 hours with an observed-hour fraction near 1. Those are not encounters. The 168-hour threshold matches the membership builder's own 7-day reassignment rule, so the two agree by design. They occur in Bronze–Magenta, Copper–LapisSplinter, Copper–Lilac, LapisSplinter–Lilac and Maroon–Sapphire — and the Copper–LapisSplinter and LapisSplinter–Lilac windows are identical, which is a three-way fusion rather than two dyadic events. The remaining 1,689 events are discrete encounters.

What the saved rule discarded

Across the retained 5 m exposure, 42.5% of eligible bins carry zero cross-group contact — precisely the bins build_positive_episodes() removes before measuring integration. The worked example is blunter still: Lapis–LapisSplinter event E0080, 19–21 May 2026, structural span 42 h, 517 eligible bins, of which the saved rule keeps 53. It discards 89.8% of the observed exposure, then reports the remainder as the encounter.

The reframing this forces Copper–Lilac is not an outlier dyad. It is an ordinary dyad that entered an extraordinary state. Its discrete encounters give −0.339, tenth of twelve — inside the pack. Its fused period gives −0.033, near composition expectation. The earlier pooled figure of −0.048 was its fusion dominating its own record: 93,763 of its 99,968 bins sit in the fused stratum.

So the general claim no longer rests on one case, and it is stronger for it:

General result Groups that encounter each other barely mix, and they barely mix everywhere — the deficit is large and negative in all twelve measurable dyads. Mixing to near composition expectation is a property of sustained association, a state five dyads entered and only one carried to near-completion.

Radius behaves the same way in every dyad

Using the 5 m eligible bin set as a common denominator, so the 2 m rate is never conditioned on a 2 m contact existing, the 2 m contact rate is 0.086 to 0.194 of the 5 m rate across all twelve discrete-encounter dyads, with a median near 0.14. Under sustained fusion the ratio rises — Copper–Lilac 0.262, Maroon–Sapphire 0.141. Dyads differ in how much they mix, not in how their mixing is distributed across distance; only fusion shifts that.

06  /  Phase 4 — the two stages, fitted

The stages really do have different predictors

Stage 1 is a binomial GEE with robust errors clustered on dyad (72,724 dyad-days, 317 dyads, 2,852 encounters), plus a variational-Bayes mixed GLM for variance components. Stage 2 models cross-group edge counts with logit(composition expectation) as an offset, so its intercept is the mean logit deficit; uncertainty comes from a cluster bootstrap over encounters, because overdispersion runs 12–27×. Every covariate strictly precedes its outcome, and proximity enters as two separate terms — the dyad's typical distance, and how close they were yesterday.

Stage 1 — three findings History predicts encounters beyond geometry. Prior encounters carry OR 1.76 with both distance terms in the model, and 1.57 [1.27, 1.93] when restricted to dyads already within 5 km — so repeated contact between particular pairs is not a restatement of range overlap. Encounter propensity is a dyad property: the dyad variance component is 1.52–1.59 across prior scales while the shared group component shrinks from 0.12 to 0.02 as the prior tightens, meaning it is not identified. Collar coverage does not manufacture encounters — both coverage intervals span 1, in both samples.

Two coefficients that did not survive specification

Prior encounters is calendar time. Within a dyad it correlates 0.285 with elapsed days; adding an explicit time term moves it to −0.082 [−0.289, +0.060]. There is no support for mixing declining with encounter history.

Distance and coverage effects at Stage 2 are between-dyad confounds. Both are null within dyad and both look real once dyad effects are dropped — pre-event distance to −0.123 [−0.216, −0.018], coverage to −0.389 [−0.487, −0.295] — while overdispersion doubles from 12.8 to 27.1. A mixing model without dyad structure reports dyad identity as if it were a covariate.

The sustained-association contrast, confirmed with a caveat

Unconditioned and within dyad, is_sustained is +1.376 [+0.338, +1.543]: a sustained association mixes about 1.4 logit units closer to expectation than the same dyads' discrete encounters. Adding exposure to that model collapses it to −0.109 [−0.789, +0.462] — a second instance of the collinearity trap, since sustained events are by construction the highest-exposure ones, exactly as they are the longest. It rests on 16 events in 3 dyads and the interval is strongly asymmetric, so Phase 3's per-dyad description stays the primary evidence and this is a confirmation, not an independent test.

What this settles Result 4 is now a positive finding rather than an absence. Geometry and history drive whether groups meet; neither predicts how much they mix, where duration is the only robust term. That the two stages have genuinely different predictors is what justified separating them in the first place.
07  /  Limits and next step

What Phases 0–4 did not settle

  • Fine-scale coverage is 12 of 68 structural dyads. The gradient rests on dyads for which an upstream 2-minute product exists. Extending it means regenerating that product for more dyads, which was not attempted here.
  • Eligible bins are bins with at least one within-radius edge in a shared cluster. A fully unconditioned denominator needs the 2-minute GPS join; the radius-nested comparison bounds how much this matters but does not remove the conditioning.
  • The composition-preserving shuffle is the reference throughout. The within-origin reference used in the Copper–Lilac analysis is a different estimator; the two are not interchangeable.
  • Demographic group size is still absent. Phases 2 and 3 use observed collar counts, correctly labeled as such. Phase 4 must not reuse them as group size.
  • Nothing here is a model. These are repaired descriptive quantities with uncertainty.
  • Calibration does not transfer to unseen dyads (slope 0.540), and Stage 2 is within-dyad only — 12 dyads cannot carry a between-dyad claim.
  • The mixed model is variational Bayes, a mean-field approximation. Its variance components are informative and prior sensitivity is reported, but pymc has no compiler in this environment and no MCMC was attempted, so nothing here is a hierarchical Bayesian fit.

    • Phase 5 entry conditions are met

    Both stages now have a frozen sample, a specification that survives its own sensitivity checks, and a documented set of dead coefficients. No ecological covariate is in either model yet — NDVI, rainfall and shared concentrated resources belong to Phase 5, together with the matched fission model that makes the scarcity-fragments / abundance-connects prediction falsifiable. The bar is already set: the seasonal term here passed in the full sample and failed the within-5 km restriction, so any ecological effect must clear that same test.
